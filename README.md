## Common questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] →
Subscriptions → PocketMind AI → Cancel.

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app: Settings → Delete account & all data. This removes
everything from your device and our servers.

**Do I need to connect my bank?**
No. PocketMind never asks for bank credentials.

**Why is there a monthly limit on AI features?**
Free accounts include a monthly allowance of AI parsing, voice
notes, receipt scans, and advisor questions. PocketMind Pro
removes the limits.
